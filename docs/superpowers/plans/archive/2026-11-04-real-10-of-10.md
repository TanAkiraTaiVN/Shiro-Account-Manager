**Goal:** Take the project from "gates pass" (8/10) to 10/10 by moving dialog logic out of code-behind into testable ViewModels, fixing the defects found in the 2026-11-04 review, raising measured coverage to ≥ 75% line / ≥ 60% branch, and adding headless-UI and Nexus end-to-end tests.
